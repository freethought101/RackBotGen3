(40 s) Four motors do all the moving: two for the wheels, one to lift up and down, and one to move in and out. To hold a drive, a small servo turns a finger down into a gap in the drive's handle. The same finger pulls and pushes, so we never squeeze the drive. To dock, the camera brings the robot close. Then a V-shaped guide puts it in the exact place, within 2 millimetres.
