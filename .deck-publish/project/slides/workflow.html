(40 s) One job has seven steps. The robot takes a good drive from its home station and drives to the rack. It checks the broken drive's label and pulls it out. Then it moves down 45 millimetres and pushes the good drive into the same place. It goes home, leaves the broken drive there, and charges. Because the robot has two slots, one trip is enough. In our simulation, one job takes 107 seconds.
