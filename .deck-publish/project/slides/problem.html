A failed drive shouldn’t wait for a technician

Every failed drive in a data center means a technician trip, and a RAID array running degraded until the swap is done.

Corvin does the swap itself: the dashboard flags the drive, a mobile robot fetches a spare, swaps it, and returns the failed one for RMA.

(40 s) Today, when a hard drive breaks, a person must walk to the rack to change it. Until they arrive, the data is at risk: if one more drive breaks, data can be lost. Corvin does this job instead. We have two goals: one swap in three minutes or less, and 48 out of 50 swaps with no help from people. We will test both on the real robot.
