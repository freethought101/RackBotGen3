(45 s) Our system has three parts. The rack is controlled by a Delta PLC. It reads the bay switches, the emergency stop and the warning light, and it turns the charging power on and off. On the robot, a Raspberry Pi is the brain: it runs the camera, the job steps and the dashboard. It talks to the PLC over the local network, with no internet. Our own ESP32 board controls the motors.
