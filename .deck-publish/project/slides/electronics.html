(40 s) We designed our own circuit board. It holds an ESP32 and four motor drivers. A 7.5 amp fuse protects everything after the battery. A sensor measures how much energy each swap uses. The emergency stop cuts the motor power, and a broken wire also stops the motors. We tested three risky cases in simulation, and all of them are safe.
